# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project welcoming and free from harassment.

## Our Standards

Be respectful, considerate, and constructive. Harassment, personal attacks, and discriminatory behavior are unacceptable.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers will clarify and enforce these standards fairly and consistently.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainers through the repository's contact channel. Maintainers will review reports and protect the reporter's privacy where possible.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers may issue a correction, warning, temporary ban, or permanent ban according to the severity and pattern of the behavior.
